Common questions

Each answer links straight to the part of the guide that explains it.

  • Can you use a Joker in a pair?No. Jokers only work in groups of three or more.
  • How many tiles do you start with?13. East, the dealer, starts with 14.
  • What makes a hand dead?Usually a wrong tile count, an impossible exposure or an illegal Joker.
  • What does “Soap” mean?The White Dragon. On the card it also stands for zero.
  • What is a blind pass?Passing incoming tiles on unseen, on the last pass of a Charleston.
  • How do you exchange a Joker?On your turn, give the natural tile and take the Joker.
  • Who pays when someone wins?Everyone pays the hand value; the discarder, or everyone on a self-drawn win, pays double.
