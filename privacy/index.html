Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

MahjongKit ("we", "us") is an educational website at mahjongkit.com. This policy explains what information the site may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language.

Information we collect

MahjongKit has no accounts, no comments and no forms that store your data. You can read every page without telling us who you are. The site does not track what you type into the search box; search runs entirely in your browser.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps) for security and troubleshooting.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how the site performs. Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as your approximate location, device and browser, and the pages you visit. This data is aggregated and does not identify you by name.

Advertising

MahjongKit may display advertising, including through Google AdSense. When advertising is enabled:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and other identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Other advertising partners, if any, will be listed here when they are added.

Consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before setting advertising or analytics cookies, using a consent management platform. You can change your choices at any time through that tool.

Affiliate links

Some product links on gear pages are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may set a cookie so that MahjongKit can be credited with a commission. This does not change the price you pay. See our affiliate disclosure.

Third-party sites

We link to other websites, including the National Mah Jongg League, online Mahjong platforms and retailers. Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.

Children

MahjongKit is a general-audience site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Use the consent tool (where shown) to decline advertising and analytics cookies.
  • Opt out of personalized ads through the links above.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes, for example when advertising or analytics are turned on. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@mahjongkit.com. Site operator: Mark.
